# Relaybase Worker 0.1.10

Pairs with Relaybase web **0.2.0** / desktop **0.1.10**.

## Highlights

- **Subdomain Parent Zone Resolution**: Supports email routing and sending on subdomains (e.g. `mail.kloy.app`) by automatically resolving and mapping to the parent apex zone (`kloy.app`) in Cloudflare.
- **Subdomain Email Routing DNS Automation**: Automatically provisions and manages Cloudflare Email Routing MX and TXT records on the parent apex zone for subdomain hosts.
- **Worker Version Sync**: Automated deployment pinning for `WORKER_VERSION` from `package.json`.

## Changes

- Add `findParentZoneForDomain` and `domainUsesParentZone` helper routines in `src/lib/cloudflare/zone-domain.ts`.
- Automatically upsert Cloudflare Email Routing MX and SPF DNS records for subdomain hosts during `ensureInboundRouting`.
- Optimize zone resolution in `enrichDomainSummariesWithCloudflare` and `consoleDomains` router.
- Synchronize `--var WORKER_VERSION:<version>` in `scripts/deploy.mjs`.
